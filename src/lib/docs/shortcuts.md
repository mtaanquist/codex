# Keyboard shortcuts

Codex keeps to a small set of shortcuts, shown for your keyboard: Cmd and
Option on a Mac, Ctrl and Alt everywhere else.

## Anywhere

- **Ctrl+K**: open the command palette to jump to any universe, story, scene, or
  entity, or run a command (new scene, switch view, focus mode, and more).
- **Ctrl+Alt+N**: on a story's Write page, open the quick-note card to jot a
  note without leaving the editor. Ctrl+Enter saves it, Esc closes it.
- **Arrow keys** move through any open menu, and Home and End jump to its first
  and last item. Esc closes the menu.

## In the editor

- **Ctrl+B**: bold the selection.
- **Ctrl+I**: italicise the selection.
- **Ctrl+F**: find and replace within the scene. The buttons under the panel widen it to
  the chapter or the whole story.
- **Arrow keys** on the formatting bar move between its tools, and Home and End
  jump to the first and last. Tab moves on to the text.
- **Ctrl+Alt+M**: mark the selected text as a TODO. A line that begins with
  `TODO:` becomes one on its own, no shortcut needed. Words that are not saved
  yet, for example while the scene cannot save, cannot be marked until they are;
  if the selection also takes in saved words, only those are marked. If the
  scene was changed in another window and you have edits here that are not
  saved, choose which version to keep first, then mark the words again. If you
  have no unsaved edits, the editor shows the newer version instead; select the
  words and mark them again.
- **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Shift+Z**: undo and redo. Once the editor shows a
  newer version from another window, undo cannot go back past it.
- **Ctrl+J**: ask the Assistant to continue the passage at the cursor (when the
  Assistant is set up). Tab accepts the grey suggestion; Esc dismisses it.

In the continuous "read the whole story" view, the up and down arrows carry the
cursor across scene boundaries, so you can move through the book as one document.

## Finding more

Most actions also live in the command palette (Ctrl+K) and in the menus and
buttons around the editor, so you do not have to memorise shortcuts to reach
them.
